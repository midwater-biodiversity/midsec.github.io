Where to stay


We have reserved Motel units at Dunedin Holiday Park - there are 24 beds available in a variety of configurations. Each unit contains 3-5 beds split between 2-3 bedrooms, a private bathroom, and a fully equipped kitchen.

Click here for room details

Guests that want to use these rooms can pay the workshop organizers. A bed costs NZD $600.75 for the full 9 nights, or NZD $66.75 per night. If you need a particular bed configuration please let us know when you register.


There are also some smaller hotels and B&Bs in Portobello, which is a 5 minute drive from the workshop venue. Guests who would prefer to stay there will need to arrange their own accommodation.
