Bioblitz 2027 - Dunedin, New Zealand

1 February – 10 February

The 2027 New Zealand bioblitz will be a midwater capacity building workshop for up to 18 trainees on how to sample, recognize and work with midwater animals. We want to prepare the trainees for what it is like to participate in an oceanic cruise, specifically collecting and recognizing a variety of animals, working with them while alive, data tracking though multiple processes, imaging, tissue sampling and vouchering. We are also planning some associated experimental work with live animals like we would do at sea - physiology, behavior, advanced imaging, etc.

The idea is to go out twice in 7 days (see Program page), complete midwater and plankton tows, and bring the catch to the New Zealand Marine Studies Centre in Portobello to sort and process with the experts and trainees. The water around Dunedin is shallow so sampling trips will take a full day. We expect the individuals to be on the small side of things given the nets used.
