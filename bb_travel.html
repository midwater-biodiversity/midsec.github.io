Getting to OIST Marine Science Station


Participants should arrive at Naha International Airport. OIST Marine Science Station is a 1 hour drive from the airport so transport will be required.


BUSES

Okinawa has an Airport Limousine Bus which stops at many of the resorts nearby. Information on fares and routes can be found here.

OIST operates a shuttle bus which can take guests between OIST and the Marine Science Station, as well Ishikawa Interchange (IC) and a few other locations.

CAR RENTAL

OIST has partnerships with several car rental companies and can offer discounted rates. Standard passenger vans are often available which can be driven without a special license.

Drivers in Okinawa will require an International Driving Permit which is issued based on the 1949 Geneva Convention, as well as their passport. Swiss, German, French, Belgian, Monacan and Taiwanese driver's licenses have separate requirements.
