Getting to the New Zealand Marine Studies Centre


Participants should arrive at Dunedin Airport. It is a 30 minute drive from the airport to the accommodation we have reserved, and a 30 minute drive in the same direction from there to the Marine Studies Centre.


BUSES

An intercity bus is available for $11 but only provides one ride to Dunedin per day.

The airport has partnered with several shuttle buses which are available but must be booked in advance.


RIDESHARE

Rideshare around Dunedin is plentiful.



CAR RENTAL

Several car rental companies are available at Dunedin airport. International licenses are permitted, but must be accompanied by an English translation if necessary. International driving permits in English are accepted but must be presented with the original license.



VISA

Most travellers to New Zealand require either a visitor visa or NZeTA (if coming from a visa waiver country). A visitor visa costs approximately NZD $450, while a NZeTA is NZD $17.
