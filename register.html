Travel assistance

Thanks to our generous sponsors, we are able to offer financial assistance to at least six underrepresented nations’/early career participants who could not participate without this assistance. Financial assistance can include any of the following: registration, lodging, meals and possibly even airfare/ground transportation. To apply for this assistance, please send your request budget and justification (including what other funding sources you will apply to), in the email with your bio/abstract submission.
