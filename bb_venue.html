Bioblitz venue


The bioblitz will be hosted at Okinawa Institute of Marine Sciences (OIST) Marine Science Station.
656-7 Seragaki-hara, Onna, Kunigami District, Okinawa 904-0404, Japan



Amenities

No accommodation is available at OIST Marine Science Station. See our accommodation page for hotel options, or alternatively there are AirBnBs available in the area.

The marine science station has a small refigerator and microwave available but no fully-equipped kitchen. There are restaurants available in the area, and basic group lunches will be provided from vendors through OIST.
